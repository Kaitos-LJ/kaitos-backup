# Privacy Policy: Kaitos Backup

_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

Kaitos Backup is a personal tool used by a single person, its developer. It is not offered to anyone else.

## What the app does

Once a week it uploads an encrypted backup archive from the developer's own computer to a folder in the developer's own Google Drive.

## Google user data

- The app asks for one Google permission, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`. It lets the app see and manage only the files and folders that **the app itself created**. It cannot read, list or change any other file in Google Drive.
- The app does not access email, contacts, calendar, profile information or any other Google data.
- The archives are encrypted on the developer's computer before they are uploaded, so Google Drive stores only encrypted data.

## What is collected, shared or sold

Nothing. The app has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party sharing. Data goes only from the developer's computer to the developer's own Google Drive.

## Retention and deletion

Backups stay in the developer's Google Drive until the developer deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
